04 Production

The call
sheet.

Four phases, fixed scope, dates you can plan around. You see the work at the end of every one.

Phase Unit On set You receive
01 Pre-production Direction Kick-off call, competitor teardown, sitemap, copy plan. We argue about the point of the site until it's clear. Brief & sitemap
02 Principal Design The look gets drawn — home page first, in full, so you can judge the real thing rather than a mood board. Designs & prototype
03 Post Engineering & Motion Build, motion pass, CMS wiring, then testing across real devices, screen readers and slow connections. Staging site
04 Release Release Go live, search and analytics configured, a walkthrough recorded for your team, then 30 days of aftercare. Live site & handover

05 The Studio

A wright
is a maker.

Shipwright. Playwright. Wheelwright. The word attaches itself to people who build things that carry other people somewhere.

That's the half of our name we care about. Wright Click Studio builds the thing that carries a stranger to the point where they trust you enough to call. Most of the web has stopped trying to do that — the same five layouts, the same stock photograph of a handshake, the same forgettable arrival.

We work with a small number of clients at a time and give each one the full production: a point of view, a design drawn from scratch, code written by hand, and a launch that's actually planned. If you want a site up by Friday for a few hundred dollars, we're the wrong studio and we'll tell you so on the call.

  • Custom or nothing. No themes, no page builders, no recycled layout with your colours poured over it.
  • Fast is a feature. Speed and accessibility are scoped in from day one, never bolted on after.
  • You own everything. Code, content, domains, accounts. No hostage situations.
