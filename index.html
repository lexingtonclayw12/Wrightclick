Our Story

32 Years of Purpose

From a living room gathering to a thriving community.

1994

The Beginning

Pastor James and Sarah Wright started Grace & Glory in their living room with 12 people and a vision to build a church that felt like family.

2003

First Building

After years of growth in rented spaces, we moved into our first permanent home — a 500-seat sanctuary in downtown Greenville.

2012

Global Reach

Launched our first international mission partnership, beginning a movement that now spans 120+ countries.

2019

New Campus

Opened our 2,500-seat main campus with state-of-the-art facilities, a kids center, and a community café.

Today

Still Growing

Over 2,400 members, 15 active ministries, and a second campus launching later this year. The best is yet to come.
