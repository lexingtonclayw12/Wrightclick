WrightClick Clip Poster

WrightClick Clip Poster is a private, single-account tool that publishes short video clips of Williamson’s Chapel United Methodist Church’s own worship services to the church’s own social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok).

It is operated by the church’s media volunteer team, is not distributed to the public, and does not collect, store, or process any data from anyone.
