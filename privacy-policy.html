Privacy Policy

Wright Click Studio · wrightclickstudio.com
Last updated: 27 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information you give us through this website. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

Only what you type into the production brief form on this site:

  • Your name, and your company if you give one
  • Your email address
  • The project format and timeline you select
  • Whatever you write in the brief field

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents, or any special-category personal data through this website. Please don't send them to us through the form.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, scope the work, and — if we end up working together — to run the project. That's the whole purpose. We do not sell, rent, or share your details with anyone for marketing, and we do not add you to a mailing list because you contacted us.

Where it goes

Form submissions arrive in our email inbox, delivered by our email provider, and may pass through a form-handling service on the way. Those providers process the message on our behalf and are bound to keep it confidential. Enquiries are kept while they are live and for a reasonable period afterwards for our business records, then deleted.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics. It stores one small flag in your browser's session storage so the opening title sequence doesn't replay every time you navigate. That flag holds no personal information, never leaves your browser, and disappears when you close the tab.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and standard request headers, as it does for any file fetched from another server. Google states that it does not use these requests to build advertising profiles. If you would rather this did not happen, ask us and we will self-host the fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@wrightclickstudio.com and we will act on it promptly and at no cost. If you are in the UK, EU, or a US state with its own privacy law, those rights are yours by statute and this paragraph does not limit them.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfect, and we will tell you directly if anything happens that affects your information.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
