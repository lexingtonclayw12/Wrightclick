Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026
Operated by: Williamson's Chapel United Methodist Church, Mooresville, NC

This application ("Clip Poster") is an internal tool used solely by Williamson's Chapel UMC staff to publish short video clips from our worship services to social media platforms, including TikTok.

What we collect

This application does not collect, store, or process personal information from viewers or end users. It operates entirely on behalf of Williamson's Chapel UMC's own social media accounts.

TikTok API usage

We use the TikTok Content Posting API to upload video content to our own TikTok account. We access only the video.publish scope. We do not access, store, or share any TikTok user data belonging to third parties.

Data storage

API credentials are stored locally on church-owned equipment and are not transmitted to any third-party service.
