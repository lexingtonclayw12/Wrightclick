Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

1. Summary

WrightClick Clip Poster (“the App”) is a private, single-account tool that posts Williamson’s Chapel United Methodist Church’s own video clips to the Church’s own social media accounts. The App does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information from any member of the public.

2. What data the App handles

  • Church video content — worship service recordings owned by the Church, processed on the Church’s own computer.
  • Access credentials — authentication tokens for the Church’s own social media accounts, stored locally on the Church’s computer and never transmitted anywhere except to the respective platform (Meta or TikTok) to publish content.

3. What the App does not do

  • It has no public users and collects nothing from viewers of the posted content.
  • It does not read, store, or process data belonging to any third party.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

4. Data retention

Video files and transcripts remain on the Church’s own computer and may be deleted at any time. Posted clips are governed by the privacy policies of the platforms they are posted to.

5. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the Church via willchapumc.org.
