About Your Instructor

Music is a lifelong conversation between the hands and the heart.

Carrie Wright holds a B.S. in Music Education from Southern Methodist University (1999), with concentrations in piano and clarinet. She has been teaching piano since high school — a passion that has only deepened over the decades.

Carrie teaches students of all ages and levels, from complete beginners discovering the keyboard for the first time to experienced players looking to refine their technique. Her approach blends classical fundamentals with genuine encouragement, creating a studio where every student feels confident and inspired.

Repertoire & Supplies

Carrie uses the Faber Piano Adventures series as the primary curriculum — one of the most respected and comprehensive piano methods available. It provides a clear, musical, and enjoyable progression from beginner through advanced levels.

Students will also need access to a piano or keyboard at home to practice between sessions. Carrie can advise on suitable instruments for every budget.
